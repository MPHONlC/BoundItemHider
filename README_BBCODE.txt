[SIZE="5"][COLOR="SeaGreen"]Bound Item Hider[/COLOR][/SIZE]
[COLOR="Gray"][i]Hides character-bound items on the Deposit tab of the bank and house storage chests, where they can never go. Works with keyboard and console.[/i][/COLOR]
